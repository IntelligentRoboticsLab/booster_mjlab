In your browser

The AMP velocity policy running live in MuJoCo, steered with a joystick, keyboard or gamepad

The exported policy runs at 50 Hz on MuJoCo compiled to WebAssembly, entirely in your browser.

walk / turn

Push the stick forward to walk and sideways to turn, or click the scene and use WASD to move and QE to turn. Gamepads work too. Drag with the mouse to orbit the camera.

Motion references

Locomotion clips from LAFAN1, retargeted onto the parallel-ankle K1. They drive the AMP motion prior, and the built-in motion viewer lets you browse, play back and edit clips before training.
